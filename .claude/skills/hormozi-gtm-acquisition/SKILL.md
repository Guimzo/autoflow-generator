# GTM: Customer Acquisition & Lead Generation (Hormozi Playbook)

This skill distills Alex Hormozi's tactical lead-gen and customer-acquisition content into a decision engine an AI agent can use to write campaigns, pick channels, and diagnose pipeline problems.

---

## When to use

Trigger this skill when the user says any of:

- "I need more leads" / "pipeline is dry" / "low pipeline"
- "How do I get customers for [business]?"
- "Should I run ads or do outreach?"
- "My CAC is too high"
- "Design me a lead magnet / opt-in / freebie"
- "Write me a cold email / DM / referral script"
- "How do I set up an affiliate program?"
- "My offer converts but traffic sucks" (or vice versa)
- "I'm a new business with no audience"
- "How do I get referrals?"
- "I want to scale a local/SMB/service business"
- Any question about top-of-funnel, opt-in rates, conversion rates, ad creative, warm/cold outreach

Do NOT use this skill for: offer/pricing design (use offers skill), retention/churn, sales call closing scripts (use sales skill), brand/content production quality.

## The Core Framework

### 1. The Lead Gen Equation

> Leads = Traffic x Conversion Rate x (1 + Referral Multiplier)

You can only move three levers: get more traffic, convert a higher % of it, or amplify with word-of-mouth. Most businesses grab the first lever. Hormozi's bias: **convert the traffic you already have first** (lead magnets), then multiply it (referrals), then buy more (paid/content).

### 2. The Four Channel Buckets (Core Four)

| Channel | 1-to-1 | 1-to-many |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm** (people who know you) | Warm outreach | Post content / free value |
| **Cold** (people who don't) | Cold outreach | Run paid ads |

Every acquisition activity fits one of these four boxes. You should be active in at least one per column, and graduate to all four at scale.

### 3. The "More Better New Reactivate" stack

In priority order when pipeline is dry:
1. **More** of what's already working (double the output of your current best channel)
2. **Better** (split-test the headline, hook, thumbnail, offer, integration)
3. **New** channel (open a new box from the Core Four)
4. **Reactivate** dormant leads and past customers

### 4. LTV is the moat (The Arms Race)

> "Advertising only becomes more expensive over time. LTV is the arms race."

CAC is roughly equal across competitors in a category. Winners win on LTV. If you can't outspend, you can't outbuy leads. Fix monetization before blaming lead cost.

### 5. Pre > Post (Leverage sits upstream)

> "An ounce of pre is worth a pound of post."

Time spent on ideation (hook, headline, offer, thumbnail concept, ad angle) returns 10-100x what time spent on production/polish returns. The idea itself is the leverage point.

### 6. Need-to-Believes

Every purchase requires the prospect to believe N things. Fewer need-to-believes = faster sales. Re-engineer offers to minimize beliefs required. Amazon's "upload a product and make money" is the gold standard (near-zero need-to-believes).

### 7. Sell in a vacuum

Two ways to remove competition: **sell where no one else sells** (new audience/pond) or **sell what no one else sells** (unique product/feature).

### 8. "What would it take to be #1?"

Instead of incrementalism (10% better), assume the #1 outcome and reverse-engineer. Often reveals the real constraint is a single asset (content, brand, deal flow) the user isn't prioritizing.

## Channels & Plays

### Channel 1: Warm Outreach (1-to-1)

**When to use:** Day 0. Zero budget. Service business. Need customers this week. Under $1M/yr.

**Core play:**
- List everyone you know who could plausibly buy, refer, or introduce (past colleagues, past customers, friends of friends, LinkedIn 1st-degree)
- Message them individually with: context + specific ask + easy opt-out

**Benchmark:** 10-30% response rate if personal; 30-50%+ will engage if there's existing rapport.

**Script template:** see Scripts section.

---

### Channel 2: Cold Outreach (1-to-1)

**When to use:** Well-defined ICP, B2B, ticket >$500, you can identify 500+ prospects by list.

**Mechanics:**
- Build a list (Apollo, Sales Nav, scraped)
- Multi-channel: email + LinkedIn + (phone where appropriate)
- 3-7 touch sequence
- Lead with the lead magnet, not the sales pitch

**Benchmarks (Hormozi-aligned ballpark):**
| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Open rate (email) | 40-60% |
| Reply rate | 2-8% |
| Positive reply | 30-50% of replies |
| Booked meetings per 1000 sent | 5-20 |
| Meeting -> close | 15-30% |

**Rule:** If you don't have a lead magnet, you're asking for a meeting cold. Offer the free audit/tool/teardown first.

---

### Channel 3: Paid Ads (1-to-many, cold)

**When to use:** Offer is proven (you've closed 10+ organic customers), you have cash for a 60-day test, LTV supports >$50 CAC.

**Order of operations:**
1. Proven offer (cash in the door from organic/warm)
2. Landing page with lead magnet (not "book a call")
3. Retargeting + email nurture on the back end
4. Start paid

**Creative priority (pre > post):**
1. **Offer** (the thing you're giving away) — biggest split-test winner
2. **Headline / hook** (first 3 seconds, first line)
3. **Image / thumbnail** — test multiple
4. **Third-party integration / funnel tool** — underrated, can 2-5x

**Benchmarks (directional, vary by industry):**
| Metric | Range |
|---|---|
| CTR | 1-3% |
| Opt-in rate (landing page) | 20-40% for good lead magnet |
| Cost per lead | $5-$50 SMB / $50-$300 B2B |
| Lead -> appointment | 20-40% |
| Appointment -> sale | 15-35% |
| CAC payback | target <90 days |

**Rule:** First campaign in a market always does the best. Stop chasing the first-time high. Build LTV to absorb rising CPMs.

---

### Channel 4: Content (1-to-many, warm-building)

**When to use:** Long-term brand moat. You want inbound deal flow. You can commit 12+ months.

**Format priority per Hormozi's own arc:**
- Short-form clips (IG/TikTok/Shorts/X) for top of funnel
- Long-form YouTube + podcast for depth + trust
- Written (book, newsletter) for ultimate credibility

**Pre-production rules:**
- Invest in the idea/angle, not the polish
- Titles/thumbnails get split-tested
- Every piece ends with a CTA

---

### Channel 5: Referrals (warm, amplifier)

**When to use:** Always on. Starting week 1.

**Target:** 30% of monthly new customers from referrals. If monthly referral % > monthly churn %, the business grows organically forever.

**7 Referral Plays (from Hormozi):**

| # | Play | When |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Who else?" at point of sale | Every closed deal |
| 2 | Gift card play (90% off, 2-per-customer, named recipients) | Holidays / made-up holidays |
| 3 | Discount -> referral trade ("Sure, introduce 3 friends now") | Any discount request |
| 4 | Bonus swap (more service instead of discount) | Any discount request |
| 5 | "Want to know how to get this free?" + $ per referral | Post-sale moment |
| 6 | Referral at success (selfie + 3-way text on their phone) | Delivery / transformation moment |
| 7 | Spouse/plus-one request (with stat) | Intake / onboarding |
| 8 (bonus) | "Bring a friend" messaging repeated on every campaign touch | Launches / challenges |

See Scripts for exact wording.

---

### Channel 6: Affiliates / Partnerships (cold->warm via a node)

**When to use:** Your customer is already served by another business that doesn't compete. You want scale without ad spend.

**The Hormozi Affiliate Formula (the nasty one):**

Do NOT offer 20% rev share. Nobody moves for 20%. Instead:
1. Carve off a slice of your product (one unit, e.g. "3 sessions of X")
2. Let the affiliate sell that slice at any price they choose (minimum = your normal price of that slice)
3. Affiliate keeps 100% of what they charge
4. You get paid nothing on the slice, but you acquire a qualified, paid customer to upsell

**Math check:**
- Slice hard cost: $30
- Affiliate sells 3 slices for $500 (keeps $500)
- Your CAC = $90 (3 x $30) of actual work
- If you convert 1/3 into your $2k core offer -> blended CAC = $270 for a $2k sale

**Scale:** 100 affiliates on this structure > 3 affiliates on 20% rev share.

## Lead Magnet Playbook

### What a Lead Magnet Is

> "A mini offer that's a complete solution to a narrow problem. Solving it reveals another problem your core offer solves."

Lower-cost or free. Raises hands. People who pay with **time** now are more likely to pay with **money** later.

### The 3 Lead Magnet Types

| Type | Mechanic | Example | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Reveal a problem** | Diagnose pain the prospect didn't know or underestimated | Free website speed test; "4 Reasons You'll Never Have a $1M Agency"; posture scan; financial audit | Problems that worsen with time (termites, taxes, churn) |
| **Free trial / sample** | Give the thing, then take it away -> deprivation | Costco sample; 14-day software trial; 4 months free agency service | Consumable or habit-forming products |
| **One step of many** | Solve step 1 of a multi-step solution that's useless alone | First coat of paint; 1 laser hair session of 8; color analysis before full style consult | Complex/multi-step core offers |

### The 4 Delivery Formats

| Format | Cost | Scalability | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Software / tool** | High build, low delivery | Infinite | Assessments, calculators, diagnostic tools |
| **Information** | Low build, zero delivery | Infinite | Mini-courses, guides, templates, swipe files |
| **Services (done-for-you)** | Real labor cost per lead | Limited | High-ticket service businesses |
| **Physical products** | Per-unit cost | Limited | Events, high-value B2B prospects, books |

Combine formats. The $100M Scaling Roadmap = tool + info. A book = physical + info.

### Lead Magnet Design Checklist

- [ ] Solves ONE narrow, specific problem
- [ ] Does NOT solve the problem your core offer solves
- [ ] Solving it **creates deprivation** / surfaces the next problem
- [ ] Titled with a tested naming convention (see below)
- [ ] Has a CTA before, during, and after
- [ ] Can be qualified (drop-down, form field) to filter freebie seekers
- [ ] Delivery feels $250+ even if it costs you $25 or less

### Naming Conventions That Work

Use one of these frameworks. Test 3-6 variations.

| Framework | Template | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Number + Outcome + Timeframe | "N ways to [outcome] in [timeframe]" | "3 emails that turn cold leads into clients in 24 hours" |
| How to X without Y | "How to [yay] without [boo]" | "How to build a funnel without hiring a copywriter" |
| Adjective + Type + Outcome | "The [adjective] [type] that [outcome]" | "The lazy funnel template that converts like crazy" |
| N Mistakes | "N mistakes that [negative]" or "N mistakes preventing [good]" | "5 ad copy mistakes killing your conversion rate" |
| N Reasons Why Not | "N reasons you'll never [goal]" | "4 reasons you'll never have a $1M agency" |

### Testing the name

1. Paid test: run 3-5 headlines as small FB/IG ad sets with same creative
2. Audience poll: stories/email/text to 100+ qualified people ("Which sounds more useful, A or B?")
3. Text 10 ICP contacts directly

**Iron law:** Changing the headline can change opt-in rate 2-10x. Content of the lead magnet only matters long-term (word of mouth). Package always beats contents in the short term.

### CTA inside the lead magnet

Formula: **Clear (not clever) + Exact next action + Reason to do it now**

- Clear: "Book a 15-min call here"
- Action: button/link/text command
- Reason: any reason works. Even a nonsense reason beats no reason ("because it's the anniversary of our business"). Urgency/scarcity stack on top.

Ask multiple times. Before, during, after. Value between asks so you earn the right to ask again.

## Scripts & Templates

### Warm outreach DM/text

```
Hey [Name] — been heads down on [relevant thing] and thought of you because [specific reason].

I'm putting together a short [lead magnet] that does [specific outcome] for [their situation]. Took me [time] to build.

Want me to send it? No pitch, just thought it'd save you [pain].
```

### Cold email (lead-magnet-first)

```
Subject: [specific observation about their business]

Hey [Name],

Noticed [specific thing on their site / LinkedIn / recent news]. Most [their role] we work with are losing ~[quantified outcome] because of [root cause].

I put together a free [audit / teardown / tool] that shows exactly where [outcome] is leaking and how to fix it. Takes us ~[time] and we've done it for [social proof].

Want me to run it for [their company]? Just need [1 thing].

— [Name]
P.S. Even if we never talk again, the [deliverable] is yours.
```

### Cold LinkedIn (after connection)

```
Thanks for connecting, [Name].

Quick one — we just put together [lead magnet] for [their ICP] after looking at [number] of them. Biggest pattern: [counterintuitive insight].

Worth a 2-min look? Happy to just send it over.
```

### Referral: "Who else?"

```
"Glad you're in. Quick thing — who else do you know who'd get as much out of this as you? I'll take care of them the same way."
```

Not "do you know anyone" (yes/no). Ask "who else" (forces memory search).

### Referral: Discount trade

```
Them: "Any chance you can come down on price?"

You: "I don't discount, but I'll make you a deal — introduce me to 3 people who fit [ICP] right now, from your phone, and I'll knock [X] off."
```

### Referral: Bonus swap

```
"Instead of taking $200 off, let me give you $400 worth of [service] on top. Costs me less, helps you more, and we both come out ahead."
```

### Referral: Post-sale "free" play

```
"By the way — want to know how to get this for free?

For every person you send me who signs up, I'll cut you a check for $[amount]. No cap. I'll give you a link you can share."
```

### Referral at success (selfie + 3-way text)

```
"You're amazing. How many other amazing people like you do you know?

Let's take a picture to mark this — give me your phone."

[Take selfie with outcome visible]

"Cool — who's one person who'd want this too? Let's text them right now from your phone so they know it's really from you."
```

### Gift card play (holiday copy)

```
Subject: A little gift for the people you love (and 90% off)

[Business] clients only: 2 x $[X] gift cards for [holiday]. You pay $[10%], they get the full $[X] credit.

Limit 2 per member. Expires 30 days from purchase. Can't be used on yourself — these are for the people in your life.

Who do you want us to make them out to?

[Buy 2 Gift Cards]
```

### "Bring a friend" recurring line (every touch of a campaign)

```
P.S. This works way better with a friend — clients who bring someone get [stat]% better results. Forward this to the person you already thought of.
```

### Affiliate pitch (the Hormozi version)

```
Hey [Partner],

Not pitching a normal affiliate deal. Here's what I want to do:

You already have [audience]. I have [core product]. I'll carve off [specific piece of your product] and let you sell it to them at any price you want — minimum $[X]. You keep 100%.

I eat the [hard cost] to deliver. I don't take a cut. The reason I'm happy to do this: once they're inside, I know my numbers on upsell. It works for me even if you pocket everything on the front end.

Worth 15 min to scope?
```

### Damaging admission (truth as weapon)

Formula: `[negative truth], BUT [relevant positive truth]`

```
"We're not the biggest [category] in [area]. We don't have the fanciest [feature]. But when you walk in, you're going to forget about all that — because [specific thing].

And if that's not worth it, we'll refund you."
```

## Benchmarks & KPIs

### Funnel benchmarks (directional)

| Stage | Poor | OK | Good | Great |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cold email reply | <1% | 2-3% | 5-8% | >10% |
| Landing page opt-in (warm traffic) | <15% | 20% | 30-40% | >45% |
| Landing page opt-in (cold traffic) | <5% | 10-15% | 20-25% | >30% |
| Lead -> booked call | <15% | 20% | 30-40% | >50% |
| Call -> close | <10% | 15-20% | 25-30% | >35% |
| Referral % of new customers | <5% | 10% | 20% | 30%+ |

### Economics

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| LTV:CAC | 3:1 minimum, 5:1+ for scale |
| CAC payback | <90 days (cash-flow sensitive) / <12 mo (funded) |
| Gross margin on core offer | 70%+ for services, 40%+ for products |
| Monthly referral rate >= monthly churn rate | Required for flywheel |

### Split-test priority (biggest wins first)

1. **Offer** (biggest winner)
2. **Headline / packaging** (subheadlines count)
3. **Image / thumbnail**
4. **Third-party integration** (CRM, form tool, scheduler) — hardest to test, biggest unlock when it hits

### Team sizing (marketing)

> Fewer, better. One A-player > five B-players in marketing. Founder should drive marketing output directly until $1-5M ARR minimum.

## Diagnostic Questions

Run through these in order. Stop at the first "no."

1. **Do you have a repeatable offer that a cold person has paid for at least 10 times?**
   - No -> fix offer before acquisition. You don't have a lead problem.
2. **What's your current CAC vs. industry average (Google it)?**
   - Much higher -> fix conversion / lead magnet / funnel before buying more traffic.
   - Similar -> problem is LTV, not CAC.
3. **What's your LTV? How does it compare to the category leader?**
   - Much lower -> you can't win the arms race. Fix pricing / repeat purchase / upsell first.
4. **Of your 4 channel boxes (warm 1:1, cold 1:1, warm 1:many, cold 1:many), how many are active?**
   - 0 or 1 -> open the warm 1:1 box today.
5. **Do you have a lead magnet, or is your CTA "book a call / get a quote"?**
   - "Book a call" on cold traffic -> build a lead magnet this week.
6. **What % of new customers came from referrals last month?**
   - <20% -> you're leaving money on the table. Install the 7 referral plays.
7. **When was your last split-test (headline, thumbnail, offer)?**
   - Never / >90 days -> set up test this week.
8. **Who else already serves your ICP and doesn't compete?**
   - If named -> affiliate conversation.
9. **What's your one biggest need-to-believe for a prospect? Can you remove it?**
10. **What would it take for you to be #1 in your market?**

## Decision Tree

### By stage

| Stage | Revenue | First move |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-revenue | $0 | Warm 1:1 outreach + free services for testimonials (Hormozi's "free training project") |
| Validated | $0-30k/mo | Add a lead magnet + warm 1:many content on ONE platform. Start referral plays. |
| Scaling | $30k-250k/mo | Add cold 1:1 OR paid ads (pick one based on ticket size). Formalize affiliate program. |
| Established | $250k+/mo | Run all 4 channel boxes. Invest in LTV (pricing, product, retention). Hire A-player marketers. |

### By business type

| Business | Best starting channel |
|---|---|
| Local service (gym, spa, repair, clinic) | Referrals + gift card play + local partnerships |
| B2B service / agency | Cold email + lead magnet audit + LinkedIn content |
| SaaS (SMB) | Paid ads + free tool lead magnet + content/SEO |
| SaaS (enterprise) | Cold outreach + events + partnership/affiliate |
| E-commerce | Paid ads + influencer affiliates + referrals |
| Info / coaching | Content + lead magnet + webinar/challenge |
| Consulting / expertise | Content + warm 1:1 + referrals |

### By budget

| Budget/mo | Channel priority |
|---|---|
| $0 | Warm 1:1, referrals, free services for testimonials, partnerships |
| <$1k | Warm 1:1 + content + cold email (tools cost only) |
| $1-5k | Above + light paid test ($50-100/day) |
| $5-25k | Paid ads as primary + content + affiliate program |
| $25k+ | All 4 boxes + A-player marketer + LTV investment |

### By ticket size

| Avg ticket | Channel |
|---|---|
| <$100 | Paid ads + e-comm funnels (AOV is the lever) |
| $100-1k | Paid ads + lead magnet + email nurture |
| $1k-10k | Lead magnet + call booking + cold outreach |
| $10k-100k | Cold outreach + content-built trust + events |
| $100k+ | ABM + partnerships + founder-led selling |

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Sending cold traffic to "book a call" or "get a quote" | Replace with a lead magnet CTA |
| Giving lead magnets to everyone | Qualify with a drop-down / form field before delivering |
| Lead magnet that solves the problem your core offer solves | Redesign so LM creates the NEXT problem |
| Judging lead magnets by the content | Test the NAME first. 10x leverage on packaging. |
| Forgetting the CTA after the lead magnet | Embed CTA before, during, AND after |
| Asking once | Ask many times, with value in between |
| 20% affiliate rev share | Carve a slice, give 100% of it, eat hard cost |
| Discounting without a behavior change | Trade discount for referrals / longer term / upsell |
| Scaling ad spend before the offer is validated organically | Close 10 organic before spending $1 on ads |
| Blaming lead cost when the issue is LTV | Google industry CAC. If yours is similar, fix monetization. |
| Hiring a 17-person marketing team | Fewer, better. A-players and the founder. |
| Over-investing in production (editing, design) | Move effort upstream to pre (concept, headline, hook) |
| Incremental thinking ("10% better") | Ask "what would it take to be #1?" |
| Too many need-to-believes in one sale | Multi-step campaigns: handle belief 1, then sell belief 2 |
| Hiding negatives | Damaging admission: claim the flaw first, use "but" to amplify the positive |
| Chasing the first-campaign low CPL forever | Accept CPLs rise. Build LTV to absorb it. |
| No referral ask at point of sale | "Who else?" script on every close |
| No gift card / event / spouse plays | Install all 7 referral plays |

## Quick-start checklists

### If the user has <$1k/mo and no customers

- [ ] List 50 warm contacts who know them
- [ ] Write one lead magnet (info type, <2 hours to build)
- [ ] Test 3 names via text poll to 20 ICP contacts
- [ ] Warm-outreach 10/day for 10 days
- [ ] Offer free service to first 3 to bank testimonials
- [ ] Post 1 piece of content/day on 1 platform
- [ ] Install "who else?" close at every sale

### If the user has a proven offer and wants to scale

- [ ] Split-test offer variants (biggest lever)
- [ ] Split-test 5 headlines
- [ ] Split-test 5 thumbnails/images
- [ ] Audit third-party funnel tools for conversion (form, scheduler, CRM)
- [ ] Build affiliate program with "100% of slice" structure
- [ ] Install 7 referral plays
- [ ] Add damaging-admission copy to landing page
- [ ] Map 1 need-to-believe you could remove from the offer

### If the user's paid ads are "not working"

- [ ] Is the offer validated organically? (10+ cold buyers)
- [ ] Is the CTA a lead magnet or "book a call"? (switch to LM)
- [ ] Have you tested 5+ headlines? 5+ thumbnails?
- [ ] Is the landing page opt-in rate >20% on cold?
- [ ] Is lead -> appointment >20%?
- [ ] Is appointment -> sale >15%?
- [ ] Is retargeting + email nurture live?
- [ ] Is your LTV competitive for the category?
